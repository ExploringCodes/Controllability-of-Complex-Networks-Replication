# Controllability of Complex Networks: Paper Replication

This repository documents an ongoing replication of selected results from *Controllability of complex networks* by Yang-Yu Liu, Jean-Jacques Slotine, and Albert-László Barabási, published in **Nature** in 2011 ([DOI: 10.1038/nature10011](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10011)).

The Jupyter notebook, `Experiments_on_paper.ipynb`, contains the implementations, simulations, and plots used to explore and reproduce the paper's results. Its cells follow the paper's progression, developing the relevant concepts and experiments step by step as they appear in the text and figures.

The implementations deliberately prioritize clarity over computational efficiency. Where practical, algorithms are expressed through explicit steps and loops that follow directly from the underlying definitions. This approach makes the connection between the paper's theoretical arguments and the computational experiments easier to inspect and understand.

The repository is intended as a record of both the replication process and the reasoning behind each implementation. Numerical simulations and analytical approximations are included as the work progresses.
